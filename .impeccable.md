## Design Context

### Users

ReticulumPi is used by technically capable node operators who monitor and control a Raspberry Pi-based Reticulum station from phones, tablets, and desktop browsers. They may be working in a vehicle, at a field site, or over a slow or intermittently offline LAN. The interface must make node health, messaging, routing, radio activity, and recovery actions understandable under operational stress.

### Brand Personality

Resilient, technical, and field-ready. The dashboard should feel like a trustworthy instrument panel rather than a novelty cyberpunk mock-up: confident and distinctive, but calm enough for long sessions and urgent troubleshooting.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve the dark cyberpunk identity as a restrained field-instrument system. Use tinted navy surfaces, magenta for identity and primary actions, cyan for signal/data, and green/amber/red for state. Reserve glow and motion for live or urgent conditions. Use proportional type for prose and monospace only for hashes, frequencies, timestamps, and numeric telemetry.

Target evergreen Chrome, Firefox, Safari/WebKit, and Edge from 320px through 4K with touch, mouse, and keyboard input. The experience must remain useful over slow or offline local connections and meet WCAG 2.2 AA, including reduced-motion and forced-colors modes.

### Design Principles

1. Operational state is the visual hierarchy: readiness, degradation, failure, and recovery must be unmistakable without relying on color alone.
2. Preserve character through purposeful typography, color, and instrumentation—not ubiquitous cards, borders, glow, or animation.
3. Every capability works with keyboard, touch, pointer, 200% zoom, and narrow viewports; mobile adapts rather than hides functionality.
4. Degraded connectivity is a first-class state: retain useful data, mark it stale, explain failures, and always offer recovery.
5. Keep the initial shell lean and progressively load specialized maps, charts, radio tools, and plugin panels.
